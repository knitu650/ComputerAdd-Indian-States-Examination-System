# User Manual

This manual provides instructions on how to use the Indian States Examination System.

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1. Registration

1.  Open the web application or mobile app.
2.  Click on the "Register" button.
3.  Fill in the required details (name, email, password).
4.  Click "Submit" to create your account.

### 1.2. Logging In

1.  Open the web application or mobile app.
2.  Enter your registered email and password.
3.  Click "Login" to access your dashboard.

## 2. Taking an Exam

1.  From the dashboard, navigate to the "Exams" section.
2.  Browse the list of available exams and select one.
3.  Read the instructions carefully before starting.
4.  Click "Start Exam" to begin.
5.  Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
6.  Once you have completed the exam, click "Submit".

## 3. Viewing Results

1.  After submitting an exam, you will be redirected to the results page.
2.  You can also access your past results from the "Profile" section.
3.  The results page will show your score, a breakdown of your performance, and any certificates you have earned.

## 4. Admin Functions

### 4.1. Managing Exams

1.  Log in to the Admin Dashboard.
2.  Navigate to the "Exam Management" section.
3.  Here you can create, edit, and delete exams.

### 4.2. Managing Users

1.  From the Admin Dashboard, go to the "User Management" section.
2.  You can view, edit, and manage user accounts.
